====================================================================
STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP) - FAULT HANDLING & SAFETY MANUAL
MANUFACTURING PLANT LAB - DUAL LINE SYSTEM (LINE-A / LINE-B)
====================================================================

1. OVERHEAT FAULT PROCEDURE (FAULT CODE: ALT-TEMP-01)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Normal Operating Range: 50.0°C to 85.0°C.
- Warning Threshold: 90.0°C.
- Danger / Auto-Shutdown: > 120.0°C.
- Action: If machine temperature exceeds warning threshold, immediately log 
  a maintenance ticket using 'create_maintenance_ticket'.
- Restriction: Maintenance engineers and supervisors only.

2. SPEED CONTROL & CALIBRATION (FAULT CODE: ALT-SPD-02)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Normal Speed Range: 500.0 RPM to 2500.0 RPM.
- Maximum Allowed Range: 0.0 RPM to 3000.0 RPM.
- Action: Speed adjustments must be requested via 'request_configuration_change'.
- Approval Control: ANY speed change requires explicit two-person authorization.
  A Supervisor ('supervisor') must approve the pending request via 
  'approve_configuration_change'.

3. LINE ISOLATION & ACCESS PRIVILEGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Operators ('operator') and Maintenance Engineers ('maintenance_engineer') 
  are strictly restricted to their assigned production line (LINE-A or LINE-B).
- Supervisors ('supervisor') hold plant-wide operational oversight.
